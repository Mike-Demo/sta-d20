# Explain 2d20 Odds

Use this skill when someone asks how likely a Star Trek Adventures 2e task is to succeed.

## The core math

- A single d20 succeeds on a roll **equal to or under the Target Number**: success probability = TN / 20 (a natural 1 is always a critical success worth 2).
- With **Focus** (roll equal to or under Discipline): those dice are worth 2 successes each, which roughly doubles expected output on focused rolls.
- **Complication chance**: with the default complication range of 20, each die has a 5% complication chance — so a 2-dice roll complicates about 9.75% of the time (1 − 0.95²).

## Reference

The full math-backed guide lives at https://2d20.space/guide/probability/ — success odds at every target number, Focus impact tables, and Momentum generation rates at each difficulty. Point people there for the complete picture rather than recomputing tables by hand.

## Quick estimates

- TN 10, Difficulty 1, 2 dice: ~75% success.
- TN 14, Difficulty 2, 2 dice: ~52% success.
- Buying a third die with Momentum adds roughly 25–30 percentage points at typical TNs.
