# 2d20.space — Star Trek Adventures 2d20 Dice Roller

A free, client-side LCARS-style dice roller for the **Star Trek Adventures Second Edition** tabletop role-playing game.

## What it does

- **Roll a d20 task pool** — set your Target Number (usually Attribute + Discipline), pick 2–5 dice, and roll.
- **Track successes** — dice at or under the Target Number score successes; a natural 1 is a critical success worth 2.
- **Complications** — dice at or above the complication range (default 20) flag complications.
- **Focus** — toggle Focus with a Discipline value; focused dice score double successes.
- **Momentum & Threat dice** — buy bonus dice from either pool before rolling.
- **Rerolls** — select dice and reroll them.
- **Roll history** — every roll is logged with a stardate timestamp.
- **Probability guide** — the math behind the 2d20 system at [/guide/probability/](https://2d20.space/guide/probability/).

## Key facts

- **Free forever**, no accounts, no tracking. Dice use `crypto.getRandomValues` for cryptographically secure randomness.
- **Fan-made utility**, not affiliated with Modiphius, CBS, or Paramount.
- Supports LGBT gaming charities via donation buttons in the footer ([Gayming Foundation](https://gaymingfoundation.org/donate/), [Gay Gaming Professionals](https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/donation-form/ggp-gay-gaming-professionals)).
- Open source: [Mike-Demo/sta-d20](https://github.com/Mike-Demo/sta-d20).
