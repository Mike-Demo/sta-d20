# Pricing — 2d20.space

**2d20.space is completely free.** There are no plans, no tiers, no paywalls, and no in-app purchases. Every feature — the dice roller, the probability guide, roll history, themes — is available to everyone.

## Donations

The site does not take payments itself. The footer includes donation buttons that link directly to two third-party LGBT gaming charities:

- [Gayming Foundation](https://gaymingfoundation.org/donate/) — supports LGBTQ+ gamers and gaming professionals.
- [Gay Gaming Professionals via Zeffy](https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/donation-form/ggp-gay-gaming-professionals) — donation form hosted by Zeffy.

These are ordinary outbound links to the charities' own donation pages. 2d20.space never handles payment details.
